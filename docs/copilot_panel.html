Prespecified contrasts

#contrastisolatesprior expectation
P1C vs Bwhat the generator adds beyond rebalancingsmall / null
S1B vs Aclass balance alonemost of prior gain
S2D vs Cdoes 1e4 params match 3e7D close to C

P1 is the only confirmatory test — the direct version of "does the GAN beat oversampling", the question the prior work never asked. S1 and S2 are descriptive, unadjusted, labelled as such. Equivalence requires the 95% CI inside ±δ, δ fixed from B-vs-B spread before any C result is read. Non-significance is not equivalence; inconclusive is an admissible outcome.

A5000 community ≈$0.27/h → under $10 for the whole study. Cost is not the constraint, hours are. --metrics=none during training: 15 inline FIDs would eat 1.7 h of the ceiling. Expected FID ≈47, the measured value at 3,031 kimg on this recipe.

Prespecified rules · each costs nothing

  • Snapshot every 200 kimg (--snap=50) — 15 total, all synced and verified locally.
  • Selection runs offline: lowest FID among snapshots ≥ 2,000 kimg, ties to the earlier one.
  • Surplus rule — if the first tick beats 21.7 s/kimg, raise --kimg on the same generator, capped at 3,800. Decided from throughput, never from an FID.
  • Fifteen-minute gate — ops fail to build or >30 s/kimg → kill the pod (~$0.07), fall back to A5000.
  • No mid-run intervention. ADA climbing toward 1.0 gets recorded in the audit log, not fixed.
  • Integrity — 110 prior snapshots were silently truncated and unrecoverable. Sync continuously; md5-match locally before terminating the pod.

Leakage · already measured, zero GPU

v3's inflation came from evaluation-protocol leakage: synthetic images were merged into the dataset before the 5-fold split, so ~177 of ~205 validation positives per fold were synthetic, from a generator trained on the real positives.

  • measured Remove synthetic from the validation folds and rerun → synthetic ≈ oversampling. corrected_experiments/outputs/corrected_cv_summary.json
  • future work Generator-mediated leakage — training the generator itself on the holdout eyes — is a separate mechanism and is not measured here. Design written down (28 holdout + 83 dev, N matched at 111) so the follow-up is one command.

Attack surface

  • Colour shortcut. Six global moments reach AUC 0.9501 image-level / 0.9561 eye-level. Reported first, as a control — a reviewer raising it cites our own result back to us.
  • n=1 generator. Cannot separate a real effect from a favourable init or trajectory. Claim scoped to "for a generator trained under the published protocol."
  • Holdout reused — generator-blind, but used to quantify the shortcut. Downstream inference is exploratory.
  • No patient IDs. Splits are eye-disjoint, not patient-disjoint; contralateral eyes may cross.
  • No reference standard for the 139 RP labels. Inherited from the source dataset.
  • Single dataset, single disease. Source publication reports two Topcon models and two original resolutions — not even "same camera" is safe to assert.
  • v3's FID 40.97 appears in no log. Closest logged value is 41.69 at ~3,806 cum kimg.
